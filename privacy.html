Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 8th Flame ("we", "us"), the owner and operator of the RevEase app ("RevEase" or "the app"), collects, uses, shares and protects your information. 8th Flame is based in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. We follow Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and respect the rights given to you by other privacy laws, including the GDPR in Europe and the UK and state laws in the United States.

The short version. RevEase works without an account and keeps your information on your phone. If you create an account, your RevEase data is saved securely so it follows you to other phones. Your location stays on your device. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and you can delete your account and data inside the app at any time.

1. Information you give us

Profile and preferences

When you set up RevEase you can tell us your dietary lifestyle, allergies and intolerances, foods you avoid, foods you love, meal times, household size, budget, currency, measurement units and preferred stores. We use this to build your meal plans and grocery lists.

Health interests

You can choose health interests such as high blood pressure, H. pylori, pregnancy or trying to conceive. This is sensitive information. We use it only to tailor food suggestions, safety notes and content inside the app. Choosing health interests is optional, and you can remove them at any time.

Things you create

Your meal plans, food journey, cookbook, journal notes, grocery list, prices you enter and saved research.

Account information

If you create an account, we collect your email address and a password. Passwords are stored in encrypted, hashed form by our account provider and are never visible to us.

2. Information collected automatically

Location

If you choose to use “Find stores near me”, RevEase asks your phone for your approximate location. The coordinates are sent directly from your phone to OpenStreetMap services (Overpass and Nominatim) to find nearby grocery stores and your country. Your location is kept only on your phone. It is never saved to your RevEase account or our servers. You can turn location off at any time in your phone’s settings and choose your country manually instead.

Food scanner

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product. Camera images are processed on your phone and are not stored or sent to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, your purchase history, the product purchased and basic device information such as country and app version, so that Premium works and can be restored on new devices.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to: create and personalize meal plans, grocery lists and content; apply your allergies, avoided foods and health related food safety rules; save and sync your data when you have an account; provide and restore Premium; respond to support requests; keep RevEase secure and working; and meet legal obligations. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

5. Where your information is stored

Without an account, your data stays on your phone. With an account, your RevEase data is stored by Supabase in Canada. Some providers listed above process limited information outside Canada. When that happens, your information is protected by contracts and by the laws that apply to those providers, and it may be accessible to authorities in those countries under their laws.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and all data saved in it are deleted permanently right away. Backups held by our provider are removed within their standard backup cycle, normally within 30 days. Purchase records may be kept by Apple and RevenueCat as required for tax and accounting.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and change your information in the app at any time. You can export your data from Profile, remove health interests, turn off location, sign out, and delete your account in the app under Profile, Delete account. You can also delete everything stored on your phone with “Delete all data on this phone”. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, to withdraw consent, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us at myrevease@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your local data protection authority.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and database rules that let each person access only their own data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the authorities of a breach as required by law.

9. Children

RevEase is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Health information notice

RevEase provides food education and is not a medical device or a health care provider. Health interests you choose are used only to tailor content in the app. We do not share them with insurers, employers, advertisers or data brokers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and tell you in the app if the changes are significant.
