What you can do

Personal meal plans

Plans from 7 to 90 days that respect your diet, allergies, foods you avoid and the times you like to eat.

Health interests

Food guidance for 99 health interests, from blood pressure to digestion, with research from PubMed.

Learn Naturally

A food encyclopedia of fruits, vegetables, herbs, spices and seeds, with origins, nutrients and evidence.

Smart grocery lists

Lists built from your plan, organized by aisle, with your budget, your currency and stores near you.

Food scanner

Scan packaged foods to check allergens and ingredients against your preferences.

Fertility and pregnancy

Guides with food safety, nourishing craving swaps and teas to choose and avoid.

Free and Premium

Free includes 7 day plans, one health interest, daily ingredient and tea picks, the food scanner with allergen checks, and all safety information.

RevEase Premium unlocks plans up to 90 days, snacks and morning drinks, every health interest, the full library, unlimited scans and detailed nutrition. US$24.99 per month or US$179.99 per year, with a 7 day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Prices may vary by country.

RevEase offers food education, not medical advice. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. Always speak with your doctor or a registered dietitian about your health.
