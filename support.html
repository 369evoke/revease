Common questions

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open RevEase, go to Profile and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then RevEase, and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I move my plan to a new phone?

Create a free account in Profile on your current phone. Then sign in on your new phone and your plan, food journey and cookbook will load.

How do I delete my account?

In RevEase, go to Profile and tap Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm. Your account and all its data are permanently removed. If you cannot access the app, email myrevease@gmail.com from the email on your account and we will delete it for you. Remember to cancel your subscription with Apple first.

Why does RevEase ask for my location?

Only to find grocery stores near you and set your country and currency. Your location stays on your phone. You can say no and choose your country instead.

Is RevEase medical advice?

No. RevEase is food education. Please speak with your doctor or a registered dietitian about your health.
